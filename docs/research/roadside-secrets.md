# Roadside secrets — MVP research

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-23 (ADR 0008). Each secret is a real place; facts below come
from the linked sources and must be re-checked when the place sheet text is written.

| # | Secret | Stretch | Why it is in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cachoeira do Rio Mandi**, Vale das Cachoeiras (Nova União) | Ji-Paraná ↔ Jaru, ~30 km off BR-364 past Ouro Preto do Oeste | Tallest waterfall in Rondônia (32 m free fall); the dirt-road reward for leaving the asphalt |
| 2 | **Rondon telegraph station on the Machado river** (Ji-Paraná) | Centre | The city grew around the Comissão Rondon telegraph post; Iphan-listed in 2015; the origin of the state's name. Photo spot: the river with the BR-364 bridge |
| 3 | **Cachoeira do Teotônio** (Nova Vila de Teotônio, Porto Velho) | North | Madeira river rapids famous for traditional catfish fishing, submerged by the Santo Antônio dam reservoir; community resettled. The sheet shows the falls *before*. Neutral tone |

## Notes per secret

1. **Mandi** — private park, open Fri–Sun and holidays, booking required (goes in the sheet as
   practical info). No Wikimedia Commons photo found.
   Sources: [Portal Amazônia — Mandi](https://portalamazonia.com/turismo/turismo-vertical-conheca-a-cachoeira-do-rio-mandi-atracao-de-parque-aquatico-rondoniense/),
   [Portal Amazônia — Vale das Cachoeiras](https://portalamazonia.com/turismo/turismo-vale-das-cachoeiras-ro/),
   [Setur-RO — Rota das Águas](https://rondonia.ro.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/SETUR-Rota_das_A_guas__3_-Copia.pdf).
2. **Ji-Paraná station** — sources disagree on the date (1909 vs 1910s). Commons has Ji-Paraná and
   BR-364 photos (e.g. `BR-364emJipa.jpg`, `Ji-Parana01.jpg`); none confirmed to show the station.
   Sources: [IBGE — Ji-Paraná history](https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtb/rondonia/jiparana.pdf),
   [Iphan advisory council, 25/11/2015](https://www.gov.br/iphan/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/participacao-social/conselhos-e-orgaos-colegiados/atas-do-conselho-consultivo-do-patrimonio-cultural/de-2011-ate-2020/81a-reuniao-ordinaria-do-conselho-consultivo-25-11-2015).
3. **Teotônio** — about 40 km from Porto Velho on the Madeira. Free photos on Commons:
   `File:Rio Madeira- Cachoeira do Teotônio.jpg`, `File:Fishing at the Teotônio rapids.png`.
   Source: [UNIR — traditional fisheries submerged by the dams (2021)](https://gpbiodiversidadero.unir.br/uploads/32471044/Ictiologia/Alves%20Santanna,%20I.%20R.%20et%20al%202021_PESCARIAS%20TRADICIONAIS%20DA%20CACHOEIRA%20DO%20TEOTONIO%20SUBMERSAS%20PELA%20USINAS%20EM%20RONDONIA..pdf).
